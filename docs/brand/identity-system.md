# ZUVANE Visual Identity

## Primary identity
- Name: ZUVANE
- Wordmark: uppercase, geometric sans-serif, medium-to-bold weight.
- Symbol: a geometric Z-path formed by two aligned directional corners. It represents a controlled route through a coordinated system.
- Tagline: Work, orchestrated. People, in control.

## Symbol rationale
The Z-path communicates direction, routing, and a clear beginning-to-end flow. Its open corners imply modularity and extensibility; the consistent stroke communicates control and precision.

## Clear space and sizing
- Maintain clear space around the symbol equal to at least one symbol stroke width.
- Do not reduce the symbol below 20 px in digital UI.
- Use the full lockup where the name is not already visible.
- Use the symbol alone only where brand recognition is supported by context.

## Correct use
- Use supplied SVG assets without altering geometry.
- Keep contrast strong on dark or light backgrounds.
- Use the primary teal detail as a restrained recognition cue.
- Preserve proportions and alignment.

## Incorrect use
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, outline, or add glow.
- Do not use unapproved gradients or multicolor fills.
- Do not place on visually noisy or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Do not replace the symbol with a generic robot, sparkle, brain, or chatbot icon.

## Brand architecture
ZUVANE is the independent product identity. Integrations and infrastructure providers are presented as compatible services, not as parent brands or the source of ZUVANE's identity.
